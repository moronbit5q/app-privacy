1. Privacy Policy

This application collects basic user profile information (such as name and email address) during the login process. We use this data solely to authenticate users and manage individual app preferences. We do not sell, rent, or share user data with any third-party companies. All information is handled securely.

2. User Data Deletion Instructions

We respect your privacy rights and give you full control over your data. If you want to delete your account and all associated user data from our systems, please follow these steps:

  • Send an email request to: support@example.com (Replace with your actual email)
  • Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request
  • Provide the email address or username associated with the account you want deleted.

Upon receiving your request, your profile, credentials, and all linked historical data will be permanently wiped from our database within 48 hours. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.
